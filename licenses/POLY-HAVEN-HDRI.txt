The original 2K Radiance HDR files are included unmodified. Local JPEG thumbnails
are tone-mapped derivatives generated for the environment picker. Download URLs,
file sizes and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in environments/sources.json.
HDRIs are loaded locally at runtime; no network connection is required.
